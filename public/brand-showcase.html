Design Rationale — Why Perfect?

  • Shop storefront = local commerce, neighborhood shops you trust
  • 3-flap awning = bazaar/market heritage, variety from medical to hardware
  • Rounded square #0F766E = app icon friendly, trustworthy deep teal, not neon
  • White shop = high contrast, works at 16px favicon
  • Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 700 -0.03em tracking, premium tight feel
  • Tagline: SELECT BEFORE YOU ARRIVE 9px 600 0.06em tracking, optional >160px
  • No AI gimmicks: No gradients, no drop shadows, calm confident like Stripe/Shopify

Usage

Do: Use teal icon on white, white text on dark #0F172A, keep clear space = icon height, min 32px for full logo.
Don't: Change colors, stretch, add shadows, use tagline below 160px.

Colors: Primary #0F766E, Dark #0D5C56, Light #E6F4F3, Text #0F172A / #64748B
Font: Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter (already loaded in site)
